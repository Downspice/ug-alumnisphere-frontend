University of Ghana crest
========================

ug-crest.svg / ug-crest.png
Coat of Arms of the University of Ghana, designed by Albert Mawere Opoku
with the motto Integri Procedamus (Progress with Integrity), proposed by
Alexander Adum Kwapong.

Source: Wikimedia Commons File:UoG CoA 2017.svg
https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:UoG_CoA_2017.svg

Rendered from official University of Ghana Facts and Figures materials.
Under Ghana Copyright Act, 2005 (Act 690), official materials of state
authorities are not copyrighted. The crest remains a trademark of the
University of Ghana and is used here for the UG AlumniSphere academic
capstone.
